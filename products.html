Buying guide

Ask these before buying.

What job will it do?

Tell OHGS if the item is for home use, commercial work, site work, farm work or workshop work.

How much power is needed?

For pumps and machines, ask about power rating, capacity, fuel type, hose/fitting size and accessories.

What accessories match?

Many tools need blades, pads, hoses, fittings, glue, tapes or protection gear to work properly.
